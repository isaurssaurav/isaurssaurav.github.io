Bachau – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Bachau ("the game") is a free stacking game about the monuments of Nepal, made by Saurav Maharjan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • You don't create an account, and we never ask for your email, real name, contacts or photos.
  • The game gives you an anonymous player ID so your leaderboard scores, achievements and progress can be saved online.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ads are only personalised if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration), coins, unlocked monuments, best scores, achievements and player name are saved on your device so the game remembers your progress.

Online features: Unity Gaming Services

We use Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies, for:

  • Anonymous sign-in: a random player ID is created for your device. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Leaderboards: your player name and best score for each monument. Leaderboards are public, so other players can see your player name and scores.
  • Cloud Save: a backup of your player name, best scores and achievement progress.

Unity also processes technical data such as your IP address and device information to operate these services. See Unity's privacy policy.

Please don't use your real name or anything personal as your player name. You can change it at any time in Settings.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The game offers optional rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch to earn coins. They are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device information, IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped
  • your device's advertising identifier (on iOS, the IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. On iOS you can also change tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Online data (player ID, player name, scores and achievements) is kept while you play the game. You can ask us to delete your online data at any time by emailing us; include your player name as shown in Settings (for example "Chito Yeti #1234"). Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
