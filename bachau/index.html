Bachau

Stack Nepal's monuments, block by block, high enough to rescue a stranded astronaut.

Build Dharahara, Ghantaghar and the Nyatapola temple on the streets of Kathmandu and Bhaktapur. Time each drop from the swinging crane, chase "DAAMI!" perfect landings, climb from a sunny afternoon through sunset into the night sky, and reach the astronaut 500 blocks up. Bachau means "save me!" in Nepali.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No. The game signs you in anonymously so your scores, achievements and progress are saved online. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • How do I change my player name? Settings → tap the pencil next to your name. Please don't use your real name.
  • Are there ads? Only optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one for extra coins. The game never interrupts you with ads.
  • How do I change my ad privacy choice? In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: Settings → Privacy settings. Tracking permission: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • My scores don't show on the leaderboard. Scores are sent when you are online; ones made offline are sent the next time you play online.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → "Delete my data" (at the bottom). This deletes your online profile, cloud backup and the progress on your phone. Your leaderboard entries are removed within 30 days. You can also email us (below) with your player name as shown in Settings.
